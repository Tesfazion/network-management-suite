Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Network Management Suite collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password (encrypted), organization name
  • Network Data: Device information, IP addresses, VLAN configurations, infrastructure documentation
  • Usage Data: Login times, feature usage, settings preferences

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Log Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, access times
  • Cookies: Session tokens (JWT), authentication data
  • Performance Data: Application errors, monitoring metrics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Network Management Suite service
  • Authenticate your identity and protect your account
  • Monitor network devices and infrastructure
  • Send important notifications about your network and account
  • Improve our service and develop new features
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Data Security

3.1 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security practices:

  • Encryption: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with 10 rounds
  • HTTPS: All data transmission is encrypted with TLS/SSL
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Your organization's data is completely isolated from other organizations
  • Access Control: Role-based permissions (Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer)
  • Authentication: JWT tokens with 7-day expiration
  • Security Headers: Content Security Policy, XSS protection, CSRF protection

3.2 Data Storage

Your data is stored in secure PostgreSQL databases with:

  • Regular backups and disaster recovery procedures
  • Database encryption at rest
  • Access logging and monitoring

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information or network data to third parties.

4.2 Limited Sharing

We may share information only in these circumstances:

  • Within Your Organization: Team members you invite can access your organization's data based on their role
  • Service Providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate (e.g., cloud hosting)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your personal data and network information
  • Update: Modify your account information and organization settings
  • Export: Download your data in CSV, JSON, or PDF format
  • Delete: Request deletion of your account and all associated data

5.2 Data Retention

  • Active account data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted accounts are permanently removed within 30 days
  • Backup copies are retained for 90 days for disaster recovery
  • Logs are retained for 1 year for security and debugging purposes

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies for essential functionality:

  • Authentication Token: JWT token stored in localStorage for login sessions
  • Session Management: Temporary session data for security
  • No Third-Party Tracking: We do not use advertising or analytics cookies

7. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

8. Children's Privacy

Network Management Suite is not intended for use by children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on our website
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email notification for material changes

11. GDPR Compliance (For EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to be informed about data processing
  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Rights related to automated decision-making

12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Delete personal information held by businesses
  • Opt-out of the sale of personal information (we don't sell data)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

Summary: We take your privacy seriously. We collect only what's necessary to provide our service, we protect it with strong security measures, we never sell your data, and you always have control over your information.
