Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Click "Create Account" on the sign-in page, fill in your details including your organization name, and you'll be set up in seconds. Your first account will automatically have owner privileges.

How do I invite team members?

Navigate to the Team page from the sidebar, click "Invite Member", enter their email address and select their role (Admin, Member, or Viewer). They'll receive an invitation to join your organization.

What are the different user roles?

Owner: Full control over the organization. Admin: Can manage team members and settings. Member: Can add/edit devices and infrastructure. Viewer: Read-only access to all data.

How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page. Enter your email address and follow the instructions sent to your inbox to reset your password.

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption (bcrypt for passwords, JWT for sessions), multi-tenant isolation, and follow OWASP security best practices. All communication uses HTTPS in production.

Can I export my data?

Yes, you can export your network data in multiple formats (CSV, JSON, PDF) from the Export page. Admins and Owners have full export access.
